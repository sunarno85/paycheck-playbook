Ask for a better rate

Paycheck Playbook · US money tips
One phone call can lower a bill. Try: 'I've been a customer for a while and I'd like to stay. Could you check if there's a lower rate or retention offer available?' Polite, specific, one request. Worst case: no. Save this before your next bill. 👇 For educational purposes only. Not financial advice. #MoneyTips #Bills #PersonalFinance #USMoney
