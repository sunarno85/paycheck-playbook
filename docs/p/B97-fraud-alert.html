Fraud alert

Paycheck Playbook · US money tips
A fraud alert is lighter than a freeze. • One bureau alert usually notifies the others. • It asks lenders to verify your identity. • It lasts for a set time unless renewed. • A freeze is stronger if you're not applying for credit.
