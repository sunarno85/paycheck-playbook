Cara pakai:
1. Rekam voice-over (atau CapCut Text-to-Speech, kecepatan normal ~140 kata/menit).
2. Tiap kalimat → 1 stok video/clip, panjang total 30–45 detik.
3. Teks di layar muncul bersamaan dengan voice-over. Subtitle WAJIB aktif.
4. Musk: Facebook Sound Collection / YouTube Audio Library, volume 10–15%.
5. Tutup dengan kalimat disclaimer (sudah ada di semua skrip).

## R3 — Credit utilization (tanggal statement)

**Hook:** "Your score looks at the statement date, not the due date."

**Voice-over:**
> "Credit utilization is about 30% of your FICO score. Here's the part most people miss: card companies report your balance on your statement date, not your due date. So if you spend nine hundred on a thousand-dollar limit and pay it off before the due date, it can still look like you owe ninety percent. The fix: pay most of the balance BEFORE the statement closes, then pay the rest by the due date. Aim under 30% — under 10% is even better. Check your statement date in your app. For educational purposes only. Not financial advice."

**Teks di layar:** `Statement date ≠ Due date` → `Target: under 30%` → `Under 10% = better`

**Stok video:** "credit card swiping", "phone banking app", "calendar page", "pie chart".

**Caption:**
> This one detail can move your score. Save it before your statement closes. For educational purposes only. Not financial advice. #CreditScore #CreditUtilization #PersonalFinance #MoneyTips #USMoney

Keep it in a high-yield savings account, not your checking account, so it's there when you need it but not easy to spend. Comment your number — where are you right now? For educational purposes only. Not financial advice."

## R7 — Snowball vs avalanche

**Hook:** "Which debt payoff actually saves more money?"

**Voice-over:**
> "Two ways to attack debt. Snowball: pay the smallest balance first — you get quick wins and stay motivated. Avalanche: pay the highest interest rate first — you pay less total interest. Mathematically, avalanche wins. Psychologically, snowball keeps people going. The best method is the one you won't quit. If you're disciplined, go avalanche. If you need momentum, go snowball. Either way, keep minimum payments on everything else. For educational purposes only. Not financial advice."

**Teks di layar:** `SNOWBALL = smallest first` / `AVALANCHE = highest APR first` → `Best = the one you finish`

**Stok video:** "stack of bills", "paying bills at kitchen table", "whiteboard numbers", "checkmark".

## R11 — Sinking funds

**Hook:** "The budget trick that killed my stress."

**Voice-over:**
> "A sinking fund is just a savings bucket for a known expense. Christmas, car repairs, annual insurance, school supplies — you know they're coming, so stop acting surprised. Pick four categories, divide the yearly cost by twelve, and move that amount every payday. When the bill hits, you pay from the bucket, not from your groceries. No credit card, no panic. For educational purposes only. Not financial advice."

**Teks di layar:** `Known expense ÷ 12 = monthly` → `4 buckets to start` → `No surprise bills`
